Team Kaizen • Student Innovation Initiative

Building Resilient Tech for Coastal Andhra Pradesh

SahyogAI (Sahyog = cooperation/support in Hindi and Telugu) is conceived and built by Team Kaizen in Visakhapatnam. We experienced firsthand the communications breakdown during coastal cyclones when WhatsApp groups buckle under unverified panic forwards.

The Problem

During cyclones like Hudhud or heavy monsoon surges, emergency response channels suffer from severe information bottlenecks. Unverified, duplicate SOS messages drown out life-or-death calls.

Our Solution

An intelligent, mobile-first web hub combining Gemini-powered NLP triage, real-time spatial deduplication, and a single-click volunteer task claim board to eliminate duplicate relief trips.

Who Pays & Sustainability

B2G Annual District Disaster Licenses (APSDMA / GVMC) and Corporate CSR Grants. Rescuers, students, and distressed victims will never pay.

Data Protection & Privacy Policy (DPDP Act 2023)

SahyogAI complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 of India. We collect only disaster-relevant location and distress data. Distress phone numbers are stored in secure browser sessions and never exposed to the public internet. All data is purged upon ticket resolution.
